Program description

MAV Conversions — a trade name of San Clemente Auto Rental & Sales Inc — sends text messages from (760) 691-2588 to customers and prospective customers who have contacted us about a custom truck build. Messages are one-to-one conversations with our sales team: answers to your questions about a build, scheduling and confirming calls and appointments, and brief follow-ups on an inquiry you started with us.

This is not a marketing or promotional program. We do not send bulk campaigns, and we do not text anyone who has not asked us to on the form described below.

How you consent to receive messages

You opt in on the contact form at mavconversions.com. When you enter your phone number on that form and tick the box reading “I agree to receive text messages from MAV Conversions about my inquiry”, you consent to receive text messages from us about that inquiry. The box is not ticked for you — you have to tick it yourself. This form is the only way to opt in.

Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of any purchase or service. The form submits either way: leave the box unticked and we will call or email you instead.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. We only message you while you have an open inquiry with us, and typically only in direct reply to you.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. Contact your wireless provider for details about your plan.

How to stop messages

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. We will send a single confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and you will not receive further messages from that number. You may also reply CANCEL, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, OPTOUT, STOPALL, REVOKE or END. To resume, reply START.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, or call us at (760) 691-2588.

Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to effective transmission by your wireless service provider and is not guaranteed.

Privacy

No mobile information, phone number, or SMS consent data will be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from every category of information sharing described in our Privacy Policy.
