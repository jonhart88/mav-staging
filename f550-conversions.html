Questions we get

F-550 conversions, answered

What is the difference between an F-450 and an F-550 conversion?

The conversion is the same and so are the packages: Summit on 41" tires, Apex on 44", on either truck. The difference is the chassis. An F-450 is a pickup and arrives with its bed, which is why it carries a published package price today; an F-550 is a cab chassis with nothing behind the cab, and ours is quoted per build until our bed for it is finished.

Does the F-550 come as a Summit or an Apex?

Yes. The packages are the same on both trucks and they are told apart by tire size — Summit on 41", Apex on 44" — with the same LiquidSpring suspension, SSBC Beast brakes, bodywork and badging. What is not published yet is an F-550 package price.

Do you build on RAM cab chassis too?

Yes. We fit the same LiquidSpring suspension to RAM 4500 and 5500 cab chassis, in 2" front and 2" or 4" rear, with the matching heavy-duty rear sway bar.

How long does an F-550 build take?

Six to eight weeks once the truck is at our facility, the same as a pickup build. Total time from order also depends on availability of the chassis you want.
